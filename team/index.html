The Team

Every Graphic Novel is hand-drawn, marketed, and sold to the masses by a specialized team. But the guiding-lights of Celestial Comics are the writers. These are the brilliant minds behind every story that is pitched, voted on, and produced.

THE SUN

Kit “Kitty” Doman

Kit’s can-do attitude is infectious, and so are his stories! He specializes in writing science-fiction, dystopias, and contemporary dramas (when he’s not trying to write one of every genre as a challenge). You can tell if a story is written by Kit if it has an element so outlandish, you sound crazy trying to describe it — like a world-class detective with a lobster for a face, or a society of people with clothes stained onto their skin. But Kit really gets passionate about tragedies. If it made you cry, Kit most likely had a hand in the writing department.

Kit also doubles as the company’s face, marketing, and financial specialist. Likelihood is, if you’ve heard about Celestial Comics, you’ve heard about it through Kit.

THE MOON

Sabrina “Crabee” Doman

Sabrina’s heart is so big, it would probably give a certain fuzzy green Whoville resident a run for his money! Sabrina finds incomparable joy in the smaller pockets of fiction like romances, slices-of-life, and cottage-core fantasies. She also has a soft-spot for clowns, which is a dead-give-away for her touch in any of her stories. Additionally, as a queer woman, she can’t help but sneak casual queer representation into all of her romances; be they lesbian, gender-fluid, or trans, your favorite LGBT+ icon was probably written by Sabrina.

Sabrina also doubles as the Art Director for most comics. She does not draw everything, but sometimes she tries to. We have to send her to art-jail sometimes for forced breaks.

THE NORTH STAR

Eva “Noodle” Barlow

Eva’s thirst for adventure could not be satisfied by all the water in the seven-seas. Be it the salty brine on the wind or the whispering songs of the fairies, Eva can take her readers to worlds beyond the stars. She uses dazzling imagery and sensory-driven writing to guide you through high-fantasy, folklore, and historical fictions. If there is an over-detailed map, a structured ecosystem, or just a super cool monster design, you can bet your last doubloon that Eva is the head-writer of your favorite story.

Eva also doubles as head-research and data specialist for most stories. The only reason we sound like we know about anything we’re writing about is because of Eva. Thank you Eva for helping us not sound like idiots.
