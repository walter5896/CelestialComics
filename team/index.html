The Team

Behind every great comic universe is a creative force bringing the characters, worlds, and stories to life.

THE SUN

Kacey Doman

Kacey is the creative center of Celestial Comics — the original spark behind the characters, worlds, and stories that shape this universe. Her vision guides the tone, personality, and direction of each comic concept as it grows from an idea into something readers can connect with.

As the heart of the project, she brings the warmth, imagination, and story-first energy that keeps Celestial Comics moving forward.

THE MOON

Creative Support

The Moon represents the reflective side of the creative process: revision, refinement, pacing, and polish. This role supports the storytelling process by helping shape raw ideas into clearer, stronger, and more emotionally engaging comic concepts.

From reviewing concepts to improving presentation, this part of the team helps each story find its strongest form before it reaches readers.

THE NORTH STAR

Guidance & Direction

The North Star represents the guiding direction of Celestial Comics: keeping the project focused, organized, and moving toward launch. This role helps connect the creative vision with the digital platform, voting experience, reader flow, and shop system.

It keeps the larger universe aligned so each piece of the experience supports the stories and the people reading them.
